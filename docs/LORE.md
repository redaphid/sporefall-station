# Sporefall — world lore & canon (authored 2026-07-23, with the art exploration in sporefall-art)

As told by the user (lightly structured, nothing invented):

- A human space station / colony base on a foreign planet of thick,
  inhospitable, mysterious swamps — "a hint of voodoo, but not quite,"
  still in a space setting. Humans were starfaring at the time.
- The colony existed for one economic reason: a quality of the swamp
  water — it distills essences of things into discrete, catchable
  bubbles. A battery becomes a bubble of intense electricity. Bubbles
  were caught, sold, and used in weapons.
- Then the supply chain died — better technology elsewhere, and/or the
  empire decayed. The colony lost its reason to exist.
- Now: generational survivors getting along in a hostile-environment
  colony that is physically sinking into the swamp.

## The game (user-stated)

Roguelike, engine already running: pick up mods that combine into your
ammo — changes color and effect (electricity, split shot, any force
derivable from the bubble-concentration idea). Combinatorial complexity
from combining; color-mixing shader effects already built. User senses a
lore↔game tie but hasn't pinned it ("not sure what it is yet").

## Lore↔mechanic ties (CANON — user approved 2026-07-23: "i do really like the overarching idea so far. so we should pursue it")

1. Every mod is the essence OF a specific object (mirror→split shot,
   hornet nest→seeker swarm, thruster→knockback...). Drops = "what sank
   here"; new mods imply new objects and vice versa.
2. Weapons are bubble-burst plumbing, not guns — combining mods = essences
   mixing in the chamber; the color-mixing shaders ARE mixed essences.
3. Roguelike loop = swamp metabolism: death dissolves you and your gear
   back into essences; later runs can catch bubbles distilled from your
   previous character. Meta-progression = what you caged somewhere dry.
4. (DEMOTED, stays demoted — user finds it a bit cliché) Combination
   recipes as voodoo-not-quite craft / priesthood. Witch/preacher survive
   as visual archetypes only, not narrative structure.
5. Protagonist wears the system: bandolier/locket-cage sprites = ammo UI.
Unifier: "The swamp reduces everything to its essence — the colony just
learned to catch it."

## Tying lore to ARCADE pacing (CANON — user approved 2026-07-23)

User constraint: game is fast, hard, arcadey — new level every couple of
minutes, you kill everyone you see. Progression fast. Engine is done;
the tie must cost no engine changes.

- ENEMIES ARE ESSENCE-ECHOES: the swamp distills its drowned colony and
  exhales bubble-grown constructs of the dead (colonists, drones, fauna).
  Killing = popping the swamp's memories. Licenses mass killing, explains
  mod drops (things burst back into essence), explains per-run level
  reshuffling (the swamp redreams differently each time), keeps the
  elegiac tone at zero gameplay cost.
- RUNS ARE DIVES: descend the flooded station rings, catch essences, get
  out or be taken. Permadeath = the swamp reclaims the diver.
- GENERATIONS = SUCCESSION: each new run is the next diver in the family
  line; decades pass between runs; the colony sits deeper. Roguelike
  succession mechanizes "generational survivors" for free.
- STORY IN SUB-SECOND FRAGMENTS: essence bubbles are memories; rare
  pickups carry one five-word flavor line ("essence of: someone's
  wedding ring"). No cutscenes; arcade pace intact.
All of the above = reskin + flavor text, no engine work.

## Night-brainstorm proposals (2026-07-23, NOT canon — user reviews)

- ECHO DESIGN RULE: every essence-echo is a translucent cluster of bubbles
  congealed into the silhouette of what drowned, around ONE opaque junk
  core (a boot, a wrench, a ring). Shots pop outer bubbles; the core
  bursts last and falls as the mod drop. One sprite rule = kill fiction +
  drop fiction.
- RECALL DRONE: dead palm-size maintenance drones suspended in one violet
  bubble, LEDs still blinking their last command; pops into inert husks.
- DIVER'S TAN (lore half): bare essence stains flesh teal; stain depth =
  years of diving. (Art half already adopted in batch 240.)
- WATERLINE CALENDAR: generational hand-painted high-water stripes over
  corporate signage, cruder as they get newer — depth gauge + wordless
  history.
- DRY WALL: between-runs hub wall of caged essences from past runs; the
  meta-progression save-state as a literal light source.
- FLAVOR-LINE BANK: "essence of: the last shuttle out / a door that never
  sealed / your grandmother's dive / the dog nobody took / payday, twice /
  the all-clear siren."

## Design consequences (derived, for art direction)

- Wardrobe is HEIRLOOM SPACER GEAR re-tailored generationally into
  swamp-wear: pressure-suit liners, flight jackets gone patchwork,
  EVA-visor goggles, airlock-fabric coats, faded mission patches and
  half-remembered corporate logos.
- The world's unique visual: GLOWING ESSENCE BUBBLES — catchable,
  multi-hued (each hue a distilled essence), worn/carried/traded.
  Catching tools: net-poles, magnetic gauntlets, cages, jars.
- The "voodoo, not quite": TECH-JUNK RITUALIZED — circuit-board charms,
  vacuum-tube talismans, satellite-dish staffs, painted logo fetishes.
  Alien-swamp mysticism grown from cargo-cult memory of the empire.
- Environment: colony structures half-sunk, swamp murk + essence glow;
  palette = decayed station whites/greys gone yellow, swamp charcoal
  and bone, multi-hued bubble glow (teal, ember, violet...).

- FLAVOR-LINE BANK II (hour-2 brainstorm, proposed): "essence of: the
  stripe painter's steady year / a helmet used as a bait bucket / thirty
  seconds of real gravity / the monger's thumb on the scale / a lullaby
  in the wrong language / spectacles ground from a visor shard."
- SUCCESSION LOCKET (proposed): the protagonist wears a wire-cage locket
  holding one dim grey bubble — the previous run's diver. Meta-progression
  made wearable; ties canon #3 to the sprite.

- SPOREFALL, NAMED (hour-4, proposed): the falling teal motes are a
  *sporefall* — the swamp's exhale of condensed essence-mist; new echoes
  bloom during one; level transitions happen inside one. "sporefall —
  the swamp exhales."
- HUE-TELEGRAPH (hour-4, proposed): an echo's glow hue = the hue of the
  essence it drops. Engine tint shader on existing sprites; zero art.
- THE FIRST ESSENCE (hour-4, proposed): the bubble-moon in the key art
  is the largest bubble ever distilled — uncatchable, risen out of
  reach; charms and dish-staffs point at it. "essence of: everything we
  left behind."

- LAUNCHERS ARE STOLEN STILLS (hour-5, proposed): every weapon is a
  re-plumbed section of an essence-still — the mixing chamber IS the
  still's heart, which is why combining mods = essences mixing.
